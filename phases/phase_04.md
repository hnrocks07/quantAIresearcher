# Phase 04: Strategy Registry & Provenance

Use PROJECT_CONTEXT.md and ROADMAP.md as governing scope.

Standard gate:
1. Verify dependencies.
2. Planner creates measurable tasks/success criteria.
3. Research resolves uncertainty where needed.
4. Engineer implements with tests/docs/migrations as applicable.
5. Reviewer independently reviews.
6. Remediate BLOCKER/HIGH findings and retest.
7. KnowledgeLearning records lessons/patterns/skills.
8. Manager updates persistent state before continuing.

Do not silently expand scope. Advanced custom charting remains FUTURE unless this is the FUTURE phase explicitly approved by the user.
